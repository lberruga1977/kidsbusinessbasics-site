What Are Needs and Wants?

One idea. Five minutes.

About 4 minutes 18 seconds. For kids ages 7–12 and their grown-ups.

Or watch on YouTube: What Are Needs and Wants?

For grown-ups

A need is something you must have to stay safe and healthy. A want is something nice to have. Wants are not bad; when money is limited, needs come first. Watch together, and pause whenever your child wants to talk about it.

Talk about it

After you watch, try either question if you'd like. Talk, write or draw—there's no score to aim for.

  1. What is something you want, and how could you plan for it?
  2. You have twelve dollars. Lunch costs five dollars, and a comic book costs nine dollars. Which do you pay for first, and how much is left?
