Money lessons for kids

One idea. Five minutes.

Six short Kids Business Basics lessons, hosted by Maya and Leo, for parents watching along. Start with expenses, or pick the idea you want to talk about today. Watch at your family's pace, and pause whenever your child wants to talk about it.

You can also watch the series on YouTube: Money lessons for kids 7–12.

Expenses

What Are Expenses?

Expenses are money out, shown with a lemonade stand and a drawings example.

About 3 minutes

Profit

What Is Profit?

Profit is money in minus money out, with simple stand-and-drawings examples.

About 4 minutes 23 seconds

Saving

What Is Saving?

Saving is setting some money aside, including a share of profit, so it can add up.

About 4 minutes 33 seconds

Budgeting

What Is a Budget?

A budget gives every dollar a job — spend, save, and share — and checks that it adds up.

About 4 minutes 24 seconds

Price and value

What Are Price and Value?

Price is what something costs. Value is what you get from it.

About 4 minutes 17 seconds

Needs and wants

What Are Needs and Wants?

A need keeps you safe and healthy. A want is nice to have, and needs come first when money is limited.

About 4 minutes 18 seconds
