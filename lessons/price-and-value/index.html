What Are Price and Value?

One idea. Five minutes.

About 4 minutes 17 seconds. For kids ages 7–12 and their grown-ups.

Or watch on YouTube: What Are Price and Value?

For grown-ups

Price is what something costs. Value is what you get from it. Maya and Leo compare everyday examples, including two sticker packs, so families can talk about both. Watch together, and pause whenever your child wants to talk about it.

Talk about it

After you watch, try either question if you'd like. Talk, write or draw—there's no score to aim for.

  1. What is something you own that was really worth its price? Why?
  2. Two cookies cost four dollars. Five cookies cost five dollars. Which costs less for each cookie?
