What Are Expenses?

One idea. Five minutes.

About 3 minutes. For kids ages 7–12 and their grown-ups.

Or watch on YouTube: What Are Expenses?

For grown-ups

Expenses are the money that goes out to make or sell something. This short lesson uses a lemonade stand, then a drawings example, to sort money in, money out, and personal spending. Watch together, and pause whenever your child wants to talk about it.

Talk about it

After you watch, try either question if you'd like. Talk, write or draw—there's no score to aim for.

  1. Your sticker stand made eight dollars, and you spent two dollars on stickers. What were the expenses?
  2. If Leo buys a toy truck just for himself, is that a lemonade expense?
