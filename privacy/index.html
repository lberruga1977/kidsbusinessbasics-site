Kids Business Basics email tips: privacy note

  • Adults only. Our email list is for parents and caregivers aged 18 or older. Please don't sign up a child or enter a child's email address. If we learn a child has signed up, we delete that address.
  • What we collect. Your email address, plus the date, time and IP address of your signup and confirmation, which our email service records as proof that you asked to join. We never ask for information about your child.
  • What we send. Short money ideas for families, new lesson alerts, and occasional news about our resources, about one or two emails a week.
  • Who handles it. Our email service, MailerLite, stores and sends the emails for us. We don't sell or rent your address, and we don't share it with advertisers.
  • Tracking. Our email service records when a link in our emails is clicked, so we can see which ideas are useful. We've turned off open tracking. Our signup page for Facebook and Instagram ads uses the Meta Pixel only to tell Meta that a signup happened, so we can measure those ads. The pixel is never used on our lesson pages, our website or our YouTube channel.
  • Unsubscribe any time. Every email has a one-click unsubscribe link. You can also reply and ask us to delete your address, and we will.
  • The free parent guide stays free. You never need to sign up to download it.
  • Contact: hello@kidsbusinessbasics.com
