How it works

Three simple steps for parents and caregivers. Start with the free guide.

  1. Open the parent guide

    Download the free printable PDF. It has watch-together prompts, practice questions for ages 7–12, and answers on their own page.

  2. Explore one everyday example

    Pick a familiar scene—a lemonade stand, markers, or stickers—and talk through one clear idea together.

  3. Ask one question

    Pause and wonder out loud. What comes in? What goes out? Let your child explain their thinking.

How we teach

Each lesson focuses on one idea, so kids aren't overloaded. It starts with a story kids can picture, like a lemonade stand or a sticker sale, then uses real numbers and everyday examples. Kids get a moment to think and answer before the reveal, and every lesson ends with a recap. A free parent guide lets families practice together at home. One idea. Five minutes.

Our approach

Kids Business Basics helps children ages 7–12 explore money and business through everyday examples and thoughtful practice. Our aim is curiosity, confidence and judgment for future informed choices—not pressure to earn, spend or invest. Families learn together, one idea at a time.

Our teaching approach uses one clear, warm adult narrator. Maya and Leo are visual hosts only, not teaching voices.
